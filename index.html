How the numbers work

  1. Inspecting one pallet takes ~15 seconds (adjustable).
  2. Repairing one damaged pallet takes ~5 minutes (adjustable).
  3. Labor cost = (inspect time + repair time) × hourly rate.
  4. Each load's average is the mean of its daily costs; overall is the mean across every day.
